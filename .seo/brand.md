# A Place Beyond — Brand Context

> The voice contract. Read at the start of every `/seo` run — **everything written is governed by this file**, in every lane.

## Product

- **Name:** A Place Beyond (stylized A PLACE BEYOND)
- **One-liner (≤20 words):** The audio accompaniment to the book *A Place Beyond* by Garett Strickland — ambient music and visuals for reading.
- **What we do:** A single-page, always-dark listening room: a looping generative video background plus the original ambient track "Blackmoon," built to be played while reading the book. No accounts, no store, nothing to click except play.
- **Pricing structure:** None. Free to listen.
- **Free tier?** Yes — the entire site is free.

## Audience

- **Primary persona:** Readers of the book *A Place Beyond* (asemic/visual writing, small-press literature), arriving from the Asterism Books product page or the publisher's site, looking for the companion audio.
- **Secondary personas:** Fans of ambient/drone music; readers of experimental literature discovering the book through the site.
- **Industries we target:** Independent publishing, experimental literature, ambient music.
- **Company size we target:** n/a — audience is readers and listeners, not companies.
- **Jobs to be done (top 3):**
  1. "I finished (or am reading) the book and want to sit in its atmosphere."
  2. "I want ambient sound to read by, and this is the official one for this book."
  3. "I searched for A Place Beyond / Blackmoon / Garett Strickland and want the real source."

## Brand voice

- **Voice tags:** sparse, atmospheric, unhurried, lowercase-energy, zero hype. The site is a room, not a pitch.
- **Person/perspective:** third person, minimal; the work speaks. No "we" selling, no feature lists.
- **Forbidden words/phrases:** "revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge," "must-have," "limited time," anything with an exclamation mark.
- **Forbidden claims:** never claim a streaming release exists if it doesn't; never claim exclusivity of the audio beyond "the original track"; never invent biographical facts about Garett Strickland. No price claims on-site (the book is sold by Asterism — link, don't restate).
- **Reference brands for tone:** Inside the Castle's own site; Bandcamp artist pages (unadorned, factual).
- **Existing content to match:** index.html as it stands — the only real copy is the title and meta description: "A PLACE BEYOND is an immersive ambient music experience featuring cinematic visuals and the original track Blackmoon."

## Anti-positioning (where we don't compete)

1. Not a streaming service or playlist platform.
2. Not a music store — no sales on-site; purchases route to Asterism Books.
3. Not a band page with tour dates, merch, or a mailing list.
4. Not a generator/utility tool (no "make your own ambient" features).
5. Not an audiobook or text version of the book — the words stay in the book.
6. Not a promotional site for the publisher or designer — one book, one track, one room.

## Concrete differentiators

1. The only official audio accompaniment to the book, made by the book's own author.
2. Zero friction: no accounts, no paywall, autoplays muted video; play the track and read.
3. Original work: the track "Blackmoon" was composed for this book, not licensed.
4. Design restraint — near-black room with one player; the atmosphere is the product.
